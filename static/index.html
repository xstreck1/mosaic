A LITTLE GUIDE

From image to mosaic.

  1. Start with an image. Upload your own or explore the sunset sample.
  2. Find your level of detail. Choose squares or hexagons. Each tile averages the image beneath it. Smaller grids make bolder shapes.
  3. Give it some color. Choose a palette and set its color limit, or extract up to 64 colors from your image. Simple images may use fewer colors.
  4. Make it yours. Set the physical dimensions, then export an SVG or a high-resolution PNG. Turn on grid lines for a visible pattern.

GIFs use the first frame. Transparent areas become white. Use Crop image to choose your composition. Rectangular images fit with white margins.
